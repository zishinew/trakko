# Trakko

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Independent music producers create and operate a beat store. Artists listen, choose a license, and buy without an account.

## Product Purpose
Give producers a customizable storefront with a simple path from upload to licensed sale and private delivery.

## Positioning
Free producer accounts, no subscriptions or pricing tiers; the platform takes 8% of completed sales. Payment processing charges are separate.

## Operating Context
Producers share their storefront link in social bios or embed the catalog on an existing website. They manage music, orders, growth tools, and payments in the dashboard.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind v4. Unique customizable /username links, no producer custom domains. Beat previews, private deliverables, configurable licenses, Stripe Connect, discounts, bundles, free tagged downloads, consent capture, analytics, bulk catalog operations, collaborator accounting, and scheduled releases. Receipt email requires provider configuration. Collaborator payouts remain manual. Local credential-free demos must be clearly labeled; real integrations require credentials.

## Brand Commitments
Trakko is the user-selected app name. The user chose guns.lol as the visual reference, requesting fewer colors and a cleaner, more minimalist result. Regular-width sans-serif type, charcoal/white marketing, and neutral working screens replace the rejected green and condensed-lettering directions. Keep product behavior, simple navigation, and user-owned storefront customization.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing synthesized demo previews and six SVG album covers under public/demo. Demo purchases and figures are illustrative. No supplied customer testimonials or adoption claims.

## Product Principles
Keep selling and listening simple. Make task actions clear. Preserve private file access. Be honest about demo states and external setup. Maintain mobile and keyboard access.

The user explicitly requested removing the remaining green color scheme. Use neutral gray for platform status/chart treatments, the demo identity, and the default store accent.
